CleverBound

The product most of my building time goes into.

School boundary planning

About 15% of U.S. school districts redraw their attendance boundaries each year. CleverBound lets anyone pick a district, set limits such as school capacity, and see how an optimization algorithm would assign neighborhoods to schools, including planned housing developments.

It started as a college project in fall 2024. I have run it as a live website since then and keep extending it.

About me

I'm a recent Computer Science graduate from North Central College in Naperville, IL, and I work full-time as an Associate Software Developer at KeHE Distributors. Before that, I interned at KeHE and at Network Perception and spent more than two years tutoring computer science at North Central.

In fall 2024 I started CleverBound, a platform for planning school district boundaries, after watching a local boundary review affect families in my community. I also wrote The Developer's Playbook, a 200-page beginner's Python book that teaches programming through sports analogies.
